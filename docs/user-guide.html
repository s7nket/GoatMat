1Signing in

One account, one business. Nobody else can see your books.

Open the app and enter the email and password you were given. There is no sign-up — accounts are created for you. You stay signed in afterwards; you will not be asked again unless you sign out.

Your data is yours alone Other people using GoatMat have their own separate records. They cannot see your products, customers, stock or bills, and you cannot see theirs.

2Setting up your business

Do this first — it appears on every bill you send.

  1. Tap the ⚙ gear at the top of the Home screen
  2. Fill in your business name, your name, phone and address
  3. Optionally change the closing line printed at the bottom of bills
  4. Tap Save details

These are the details your customer sees at the top of every invoice, so it is worth getting them right before you send the first one.

3Adding your products

Each type of mat you deal in, added once.

  1. Go to the Stock tab and tap Products
  2. Tap Add
  3. Enter the name, and the size and GSM if your supplier quotes them
  4. Enter your usual selling rate per piece — this is filled in automatically on new sales, and can still be changed on any individual bill
  5. Set Warn below to the number of pieces at which you want a low-stock warning
What GSM means Grams per square metre — the weight of the fabric, which in practice means how thick and hard-wearing the mat is. A 90 GSM mat is thin and cheap; 200 GSM is heavy and lasts several seasons. It is only there to tell similar products apart, so leave it blank if you do not use it.

4Entering the stock you already own

Do this once, before your first sale.

GoatMat works out your stock from your own bills — what you bought minus what you sold. It is never typed in directly, which is why it can never quietly drift away from reality. But that means the mats already sitting in your godown need to be entered as a purchase, or the app thinks you have none.

  1. Go to Parties → Suppliers → Add and create a supplier called Opening Stock
  2. Go to Purchases → New
  3. Choose Opening Stock as the supplier
  4. Add each product with the number of pieces you actually have and the rate you paid
  5. Set payment to Cash so it does not show as money you owe
  6. Save

Your stock is now correct, and stays correct on its own from here.

5Adding customers and suppliers

Both live under Parties.

  1. Go to Parties
  2. Switch between Customers and Suppliers at the top
  3. Tap Add and enter at least a name

Add the phone number if you have it — it goes on their bills and makes it easier to find them later. You can also add a party without leaving the bill screen, at the bottom of the customer picker.

6Recording a sale

The thing you will do most.

  1. Sales → New, or New sale on the Home screen
  2. Choose the customer
  3. The date is today — change it if you are entering an older bill
  4. Tap Add item, pick the product, set the pieces and rate
  5. Add more items the same way
  6. Choose how it was paid
  7. Tap Save sale

Payment options

OptionMeans
Cash, UPI, BankPaid in full now. Nothing outstanding.
UdhaarOn credit. Enter a part payment if they paid some, or leave it blank if they paid nothing.
Selling more than you have If the pieces exceed your stock, an amber warning appears on that line. It does not stop you — sometimes stock arrives before the purchase is entered — but it usually means a purchase is missing.

7Sending a bill

A proper A4 invoice, on WhatsApp or printed.

  1. Open the sale from the Sales list
  2. Tap Send at the top right
  3. Choose WhatsApp, or anything else in the share menu

The bill carries your business details, the customer's details, every item with its rate, the total in words, and what remains due. It is a PDF, so it prints exactly as it appears.

Needs a connection A bill can only be sent once it has reached the server, because that is where its number comes from. With no signal the app will say so.

8Recording a purchase

Same as a sale, in the other direction.

  1. Purchases → New
  2. Choose the supplier
  3. Optionally enter the supplier's own bill number, so you can match it later
  4. Add the items with the pieces and rate you paid
  5. Choose how you paid, and save

Your stock goes up by itself. There is nothing else to update.

9Taking payment against udhaar

When someone settles an old balance.

  1. Go to Parties and tap the person
  2. Their balance is at the top, with every bill and payment below it
  3. Tap Record payment received — or made, for a supplier
  4. Enter the amount, or tap the button to clear the balance in full
  5. Choose cash, UPI or bank, and save

The screen tells you what will still be outstanding afterwards. If you enter more than is owed, it warns you — the balance would swing the other way, which is occasionally right and usually a typing mistake.

10Checking stock

Always current, because it comes from your bills.

The Stock tab lists every product with the pieces remaining. Green means comfortable, red means none left. Anything at or below the Warn below figure you set also appears under Low stock on the Home screen.

11Reports

Any period, going back as far as you like.

The Home screen covers today, yesterday, the last seven days and this month. For anything older, tap the 📊 chart icon at the top of Home.

FigureWhat it tells you
Sold / BoughtValue billed in the period
Sold minus boughtCash flow — not profit
Cash collected / paid outMoney that actually changed hands, including settlements of older bills
Product movementPieces sold and bought, per product
Top customersYour largest customers by value
Why it is not profit Mats bought this month often sell the next, so the two figures do not line up within one period. Expenses such as transport and labour are also not recorded yet. Treat it as cash movement, not earnings.

12Working without signal

The app keeps working in the field.

Sales, purchases, products, parties and payments can all be recorded with no network. They save on the phone straight away, your stock and balances update on screen, and everything uploads on its own when a connection returns.

  • A yellow strip at the top means you are offline
  • Unsent entries show as Waiting to send
  • They survive closing the app or restarting the phone
  • Once uploaded, bills receive their proper number

Sending a bill, cancelling one, and running reports all need a connection. The app says so rather than failing quietly.

13Fixing a mistake

Bills are cancelled, never edited.

Open the bill and tap Void bill. It stops counting immediately: stock goes back, the balance corrects itself, and it disappears from reports. Then enter the correct bill fresh.

A voided bill is kept rather than deleted, so there is always a record of what happened. Products and parties work the same way — Archive hides them from new bills without touching anything already recorded.
